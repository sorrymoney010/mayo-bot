# Live-readiness audit — 2026-11-17

Branch: `fix/live-readiness-audit`. Safety locks were **not** changed
(`PAPER_TRADING=true`, `DRY_RUN=true`, `ALLOW_LIVE_TRADING=false`).

## Verdict

**Not ready for live, and the reason is the strategy, not the code.** The code
is in good shape (601 tests pass, lint clean). But at Kraken's current fees no
strategy in this repo has a demonstrated edge, and the default live strategy
(`regime_trend@1h`) is roughly break-even-to-negative after costs.

## Edge study

Stored out-of-sample results (`data/walkforward_results.json`, generated 2026-09-26;
BTC/ETH/SOL/PUMP; 90 days of 15m/1h data, 120 days of 4h, up to ~2 years of daily) re-scored at current Tier 1 fees (taker 80+5 bps/side; maker
families shown as a range depending on maker vs taker exit):

| Strategy | OOS trades | Net bps/trade at old fees | Net bps/trade at **current** fees |
|---|---|---|---|
| regime @1h (default live choice) | 49 | +71 | **−9** |
| regime, limit entry @1h | 51 | +36 | −25 to +15 |
| mean-reversion, limit entry @4h | 27 | +111 | **+52 to +92** |
| regime, limit entry @4h | 24 | +91 | **+29 to +69** |
| Every 15m and 1d family | 15–694 | negative | −80 to −280 |

What this means:

- Only the **4h limit-order sleeves** survive current fees, and only on 24–27
  trades from one ~120-day, mostly rising market. The repo's own promotion gate
  requires ≥30 trades and positive results across folds; nothing has passed
  (`promoted: none` in every study, and in `reports/strategy_research_2026-10-03.md`).
- In the same window, the 4h trend-hold book returned +16% while plain
  buy-and-hold of BTC/ETH/SOL returned +44%. The "edge" so far is mostly
  market exposure, captured less efficiently than holding.
- Short timeframes lose because the average gross move per trade (5–30 bps) is
  far below the ~170 bps round-trip taker cost.

Outside evidence points the same way: published crypto trend-following that
does work is daily, long-only, low-turnover and diversified across many coins,
and was tested at 0.10% costs (Zarattini, Pagani & Barbon, *Catching Crypto
Trends*, 2025). At 0.40–0.80% per side, turnover is the enemy.

## What it would take to go live

1. **Paper-trade the 4h limit-entry sleeves at the corrected fees** until each
   has ≥30 closed trades and stays positive. At 4h that's roughly 2–3 months.
2. **Check your real fee tier** (`TradeVolume`). Kraken's new tiers also count
   assets held on the platform, so a larger balance can lower fees.
3. **Close BUILD_STATUS gates 5–8** (real exchange schema check, protection of
   historical holdings, independent review of the new execution adapter,
   precision/disk end-to-end on the live account). This audit did not do a
   line-by-line review of the order-placement code and could not reach Kraken
   from the sandbox.
4. **Confirm the old coordinator is stopped.** BUILD_STATUS says a coordinator
   on older code was running live; it predates every fix above.
5. Only then, the owner flips the three locks and sets
   `LIVE_RISK_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT=I_ACCEPT_LIVE_TRADING_RISK`.
