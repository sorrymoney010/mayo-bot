# Edge hunt: what actually has evidence for a small US retail account (2026-11-16)

**Research only.** No runner started, no process touched (pids 1562619 and 1562655 untouched), no flags,
keys, `.env` or orders. Code: `research/edge5/` (`hunt.py`, `ensemble.py`, results `*.json`, logs `log_*.txt`).
Data: Binance Vision spot daily klines (public, 2018-01 → 2026-10) for 20 large coins, cached outside the
repo at `/workspace/edge2-data/xs1d`. Funding history from `/workspace/edge2-data` (Binance USDT-M, 2023+).

Low-volume short-term reversal and cross-coin lead-lag are being researched separately
(`reports/lowvol_reversal_leadlag_2026-11-16.md`) and are not covered here.

## Bottom line (blunt)

1. **The one method with strong evidence that also survived our own walk-forward is plain daily
   trend-following on BTC and ETH** (be in when price is above its moving averages / past 1-4 week return
   is positive, otherwise cash). It is documented across a decade of academic work, and on our data
   2019-2026 it was up in 7 of 8 years, with a much smaller drawdown than holding. It does **not** make
   lots of small winning trades: it wins ~15-20% of trades and makes its money from a few long trends.
   It makes less than buy-and-hold in strong bull years. Its edge is **avoiding most of the crashes**.
2. **Fees decide it.** At Binance.US costs (≈10 bps round trip) it clearly beats buy-and-hold since 2024
   (+86% vs +42%). At Kraken maker (80 bps) it only ties buy-and-hold since 2024. At Kraken taker
   (160 bps) it loses. **Venue: Binance.US**, or Kraken only with limit orders and an expectation of
   matching (not beating) holding, with less drawdown.
3. Nothing found produces "mostly winning trades" with real evidence after fees. Methods sold that way
   (high win-rate scalping, grid bots, "smart money" order flow) did not survive our earlier tests, and
   the literature does not support them for retail costs.

## Ranked candidates

### 1. TOP PICK: daily trend ensemble, BTC + ETH, spot, long/cash ("TREND-6")

**Exact rule** (frozen, equal-weight ensemble, no parameter picking):
- Once a day, after the 00:00 UTC daily close, for each of BTC and ETH compute 6 yes/no signals:
  close > SMA50, close > SMA100, close > SMA200, 7-day return > 0, 14-day return > 0, 28-day return > 0.
- Target weight per coin = 50% × (number of "yes" signals / 6). So each coin is 0%, 8.3%, ..., or 50% of equity.
- Rebalance to target at the next open (limit/maker where possible). Skip changes smaller than the venue
  minimum (Binance.US $1; Kraken ≈$3.5-4). No leverage, no shorting, no stop beyond the rule itself.
- Optional variant (tested in the walk-forward family, not adopted): scale each coin to 40% annualized
  volatility (30-day), capped at 50%. It cut the max drawdown from −45% to −33% at the cost of return.

**Published evidence**
- Liu & Tsyvinski, *Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency* (RFS 2021 / NBER w24877): strong time-series
  momentum at 1-4 week horizons. A one-standard-deviation higher BTC weekly return predicts +3.16% the
  next week. https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877
- Zarattini, Pagani, Barbon (2025), *Catching Crypto Trends* (SSRN 5209907): Donchian trend ensemble,
  Sharpe > 1.5 net of 10 bps on a 20-coin book; BTC-only ~1.56. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5209907
- Monash, *Trend-following Strategies for Crypto Investors*: a 20-day momentum rule on BTC keeps a Sharpe
  of 1.25 even at 0.5% costs (1.68 gross).
  https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/3744821/Trend-following-Strategies-for-Crypto-Investors.pdf
- Hudson & Urquhart, *Technical trading and cryptocurrencies* (Ann. Oper. Res. 2019): ~15,000 rules,
  break-even costs mostly above typical fees, but the out-of-sample H1-2018 test was negative for BTC
  (a warning). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-019-03357-1

**Our check** (Binance Vision daily, signal at close t, held on day t+1, costs on every weight change):

TREND-6 by year at 10 bps: 2019 +53%, 2020 +256%, 2021 +160%, **2022 −24%** (B&H −65%), 2023 +74%,
2024 +50%, 2025 +14% (B&H −6%), 2026 to Oct +9% (B&H −17%). Average exposure 55%. Trades (a "trade"
= a stretch with any position on): BTC 108, ETH 90 since 2019. Win rate ~20%, profit factor 6-7 at 10 bps.

*DSR caveat: the SR variance comes from this small, highly correlated family of 7 rules, which makes the
deflated Sharpe lenient. Treat 0.97-0.99 as "not obviously luck", not as proof. The permutation test
(signal path circularly shifted 1,000 times against returns) is the stronger number: p = 0.003-0.004.

**Against our promotion bar (10 bps):** ≥30 trades ✅; majority of folds positive ✅ (7/8 years);
PF ≥ 1.10 ✅; beats cash ✅; permutation p < 0.01 ✅ (0.004); beats buy-and-hold ✅ over the whole window
and since 2024, ❌ in strong bull years (4/8); regime split: wins in bear/flat, lags in bull; DSR ✅ (lenient).
**This is the first candidate in this project to clear the bar at Binance.US costs.**

**Consistency with the Nov 15 NO-GO** (`archive/2026-11-pre-capit/reports/daily_trend_quickcheck_2026-11-15.md`):
that test used the vol-targeted Zarattini Donchian rule on BTC/ETH/SOL at **80 bps** from 2024 only, and
found it did not beat buy-and-hold, with 92% of its tiny $100 fills below Kraken minimums. Our numbers
agree at 80 bps since 2024 (+39% vs +42%). The difference is the venue: at Binance.US costs, with
50%-per-coin steps, fills are ≥ $8 and the edge is clear.

**Costs and US availability:** Binance.US spot BTCUSD/ETHUSD (0% maker / 0.02% taker,
https://www.binance.us/fees). Turnover is ~22-24× equity a year, which is ~$2.40-$5 a year in fees on
$100 at Binance.US, vs ~$18-20 at Kraken maker. Kraken Pro tier 1 is 0.40% / 0.80% (https://www.kraken.com/pro/fees).

**What a paper test needs:** a once-a-day runner (public Binance.US 1d klines for BTCUSD/ETHUSD; the
capit24 fetcher already supports `interval=1d`), a $100 book, logging of target vs filled weights and
of skipped sub-minimum changes, and fee books at 10 / 80 / 160 bps. **Honest timing:** ~13 position
changes per coin per year. 30 closed trades take ~15 months, and a trend strategy can lag for a whole
bull year. Paper mostly verifies *execution*; the evidence is the 8-year backtest plus the literature.
A sensible confirm/kill: run 6 months; kill if it trails cash by >15% **while** BTC is above its 200-day
average (that is the regime where trend must work), or if live-paper fills drift >20 bps from the model.

### 2. CAPIT-24 crash-buy (existing, kept as an overlay, not the core)

Already documented: `reports/edge_search_2026-11-14.md`, `reports/strategy_proposal_2026-11-16.md`.
Four-coin book 2022-26: 58-68 trades, PF ~2.5 at 125 bps, but DSR ≤ 0.22 after ~530 variants, profit
concentrated in a few crash days, and the Binance.US dry run had 0/5 limit fills. It is complementary
to TREND-6: it buys exactly when the trend rule is in cash. Keep the existing paper runner; do not add capital.

### 3. Funding / basis carry on US-regulated perps: real, but not now and not at $100

**Method:** hold spot BTC (or ETH) and short the same amount of the US perpetual-style future, collecting
funding (paid hourly to shorts when positive).
**US availability:** Coinbase Financial Markets nano BTC perp-style futures (0.01 BTC per contract,
≈$647 at today's price; ETH 0.10 ETH), https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/derivatives/us-perpetual-futures-overview,
spec https://assets.ctfassets.net/7ca8qfn907uv/36uUBfnoAFKfWNAgfZDepf/a802f97a4feeb9a85fe0845ec07102d3/nano_Bitcoin_Perp_Style_Spec.pdf.
Kraken Derivatives US perps via Bitnomial, https://support.kraken.com/articles/us-perpetual-futures.
CME micro BTC (0.1 BTC) basis. Fees ~0.02%/contract, minimum $0.15 per contract (Coinbase launch post via
https://perpfinder.com/new-perp-venues/coinbase-perps).
**Evidence:** Schmeling, Schrimpf & Todorov, *Crypto Carry* (Management Science), carry often > 10-40%
a year, driven by retail leverage demand and limits to arbitrage, https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05069.
A six-year Binance backtest: BTC/ETH ~7.7%/yr after fees, worst dip −3%, SOL lost −44%,
https://www.investwealth.com/learn/guides/crypto-basis-trade. CME basis fell to ~4.8% by Nov 2026,
https://hedgeweek.com/news/hedge-funds-unwind-bitcoin-basis-trades-as-cme-short-positions-fall-89.
BSIC average implied quarterly carry 8.7% BTC, https://www.bsic.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/crypto_carry_article.pdf.
**Our check** (Binance funding, annualized, proxy for US perp funding):

| | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 YTD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 7.9% | 11.9% | 5.1% | **3.3%** (24% of prints negative) |
| ETH | 8.3% | 13.0% | 4.9% | **2.1%** |
| SOL | 1.3% | 13.6% | 0.4% | −0.3% |

**Verdict:** today it pays **less than cash** (FDIC high-yield savings ~4.2-4.3%; Coinbase USDC rewards
3.75% with Coinbase One, https://cashratewatch.com/usdc-vs-high-yield-savings/). A single contract needs
~$650 of spot plus margin, so it cannot run on $100. Worth a *watch rule*: paper it only when trailing
30-day BTC funding on Coinbase CDE is above ~10% a year (that happened in 2021 and 2024).

### 4. Cash yield as the hurdle (not an edge, but the bar every strategy must beat)

~4.2% FDIC savings or 3.75% USDC rewards (Coinbase One, $4.99/month, which eats the yield on $100).
On $100, any strategy that cannot clearly beat ~4% a year after fees is not worth the risk.

## Rejected (with the reason)

| Idea | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| **Cross-sectional weekly momentum** (top 4 of 20 coins by 1- or 4-week return; Liu, Tsyvinski & Wu, *Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency*, JF 2022, https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13119) | Our walk-forward 2020-26: 4/7 years positive, permutation p = 0.13 (fail), 2025 −43%, 2026 −29%, max DD −75% (worse than holding). The published effect is long-short across thousands of small coins (short leg not available to US spot retail) and weaker in large coins. Our universe is today's survivors, which biases it *up*, and it still failed |
| **Weekend / day-of-week effect** | BTC 2018-2026: weekend +8.6 bps/day vs weekday +12.2 bps/day, Welch t = −0.32. Signs flip by year. No edge |
| **Hour-of-day seasonality** (e.g. "tea time", https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11156-024-01304-1) | Our 2023-26 check (edge2 idea 4): signs flip between halves, PF 0.77 at Binance.US costs, 0.61 at Kraken |
| **FOMC / CPI drift** | Volatility and volume jump, but no robust directional drift; the initial FOMC move tends to reverse within 4 h (Johansson & Vilhelmsson, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4478184; 43-event study finds no systematic return, https://exa.ai/library/publication/2nrvsbtrfv6). ~8 events a year: untestable for years |
| **Halving / macro cycle timing** | 4 halvings ever. Not a sample |
| **Exchange-listing effect** (Coinbase listings +29% over 5 days after outliers per Messari, https://cointelegraph.com/news/messari-new-coinbase-listings-really-do-outperform-rival-exchanges; https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4715718) | The move happens in seconds-to-minutes after the announcement and is captured by bots; buying after it is public means buying the spike. Binance listings ~0% after controls. Not capturable by an hourly retail bot |
| **Stablecoin depeg reversion** (USDC Mar 2023) | A handful of events; the payoff depends on the issuer not failing (tail risk is total loss). Not a repeatable trade |
| **Pairs / cointegration stat-arb** | Needs shorting one leg; US spot retail cannot short cheaply (perps need ≥ 1 contract, e.g. 500 XRP / 5 SOL). Crypto cointegration is unstable. Not run |
| **ETF-flow following** | Flow data is published after the close (T+1), and claims of predictive power are marketing-grade. Not run |
| **DCA with filters** | Not an edge; it is buy-and-hold with timing noise. TREND-6 is the disciplined version of "filtered" buying |
| **OI-flush / liquidation-cascade reversal** | Covered: mostly the same trades as CAPIT, DSR ≤ 0.19 (`reports/oiflush_lookahead_fix_2026-11-16.md`) |
| **1h regime-trend on BTC/ETH/SOL** (branch `chore/lock-regime-trend-20261116`, read only, not modified) | Same family failed our 15m-1h fee tests repeatedly (edge2, archive); its own doc says the edge is not proven and the Kraken 720-bar limit blocks a proper backtest. Daily trend is the version that survives fees |
| **High win-rate scalping / grid / order-flow "smart money"** | Failed our earlier walk-forward and order-flow studies (`archive/2026-11-pre-capit/`); order-book void reversion exists but is "not tradeable as a standalone strategy" (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7546180) |
| **Staking as a trading edge** | It is yield on a held coin (price risk dominates); it pairs with TREND-6's time in BTC/ETH, but it is not a strategy |
